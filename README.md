# RidgeHero

RidgeHero connects homeowners across the United States with independent, licensed local roofing specialists for emergency roof leak repair, storm damage assessment, and weatherproofing services.

- **Official Website:** [RidgeHero Roofing Network](https://ridgehero.com)
- **Coverage:** 109 municipalities across 37 US states
- **Core Services:** Emergency roof leak triage, wind & hail damage inspection, and temporary tarping

## Open Data & Engineering

- **[us-roofing-climate-data](https://github.com/ridgehero/us-roofing-climate-data):** Structured datasets (CSV/JSON) covering NOAA storm history, annual precipitation normals, and residential housing metrics across 109 US cities—maintained to power localized weather-risk assessments.
